About this draft

This draft describes the isolated LINK staging application: discovering people, places and activities, organizing plans and groups, and sharing content and messages. It does not describe a completed public launch. Some features are restricted or awaiting configuration.

OWNER REVIEW: LINK is the service referred to in this draft; its formal operator identity remains unapproved. Identify that legal operator, a dedicated LINK contact (not the owner's personal email), launch locations, applicable privacy rights and legal bases for processing before adopting this policy. This draft does not make a promise about selling or sharing personal information for advertising; future monetization and data-sharing decisions require an explicit policy review.

Information you provide

Account creation and sign-in use your email address and password. Supabase Auth processes credentials, verification and recovery. LINK's server receives authentication submissions and exchanges them with Supabase; LINK does not store plaintext passwords in its application database. LINK stores a provider identity mapping, email/account information, account status and session records tied to a permanent LINK user ID.

Profiles can include a display name, username, bio, profile and cover photos, interests, website, a location you choose to write, privacy choices and preferences. Your written profile location is separate from permission to obtain your device location. A profile's audience and applicable safety rules determine what other users can access.

You can provide captions, photos, comments, likes, messages, group information, activity details, plans, polls and votes. Activity information can include a venue, address or general area, schedule, eligibility rules, capacity and attendance responses. Do not put sensitive information into free text or images that you do not intend the chosen audience to see.

Location and discovery

Current Location: selecting this option requests browser/device permission. A successful request supplies coordinates for discovery and distance calculations. Permission denial, timeout or failure is shown with an option to choose a manual area. LINK does not describe Philadelphia as your detected location when it has not obtained one.

Manual location: you can select a supported city or area without device permission. The selected area name is remembered in browser storage. Selecting a map area uses that area as the search origin. The initial Philadelphia market is labeled as a default search area.

Current device coordinates and selected map bounds remain in the current discovery state rather than being saved as a device-location history in browser storage. Discovery sends its active origin to LINK in request bodies. When you explicitly search Google places, LINK sends the search area, radius and search text to Google. Changing a manual area, resetting discovery or closing/reloading the page can change which origin is used; check the visible area label.

Device coordinates used for discovery are not added to other users' profiles or exposed as live location. This is different from an address or location you deliberately put in a profile, post, message, activity or plan. Protected Activities can show an approximate/general area while withholding an exact destination until the viewer is authorized. Directions follow that exact-location boundary. Opening an external maps link is a separate interaction with that provider.

Content and social interactions

LINK records content ownership and audience, follows and accepted relationships, saved items, group and plan membership, activity participation, poll votes, reactions, comments, shares and notifications. Your Circle means reciprocal accepted follows, subject to blocks and other access rules. Saving something or receiving its link does not grant access.

Audience options vary by feature. Activities support Public, Private, Your Circle and Your Groups. Social content and Stories also have audience controls, including Only Me where offered. Public means available to eligible LINK users under the current application rules, not a bypass of the private staging gate.

Direct and plan messages are stored with sender, conversation and time information. Conversation access, requests, blocks and teen rules govern access. This implementation does not provide end-to-end encrypted messaging. Guardians do not gain access to a teen's private messages simply by linking accounts.

LINK stores interests and recommendation feedback, including Reel watch/completion and interested/not-interested signals. These are application features, not a claim that no usage data is collected. The current source does not include a general third-party advertising analytics SDK or the planned Analytics/Admin system.

Trust, reports and safety

Trust Profiles can contain an optional recognition photo, visibility preferences, membership and eligible activity history, feedback and verification status. A recognition photo is not identity verification. Email verification comes from authentication; other verification preparation does not establish that an identity, age or guardian relationship has been verified.

Reporting can record the reporter, reported content/account, category, explanation, review status and restricted review/audit information. Authorized safety reviewers have role-controlled access. Confidential written feedback and reports are not automatically disclosed to guardians or other ordinary users. Reports involving a known minor receive additional restricted context for review.

Blocks, mutes, host removals, group restrictions and safety actions create records used to enforce access and support review. Safety Center is not an emergency service or a promise of continuous monitoring or a response deadline. For immediate danger, contact local emergency services.

Family and age information

Owner-approved eligibility: standard LINK accounts are for adults 18 and older. Teens ages 13–17 may use LINK only through its Family/Teen structure with the required guardian relationship and applicable teen protections. Children under 13 are not eligible. Unknown age is not verified adulthood. This policy decision alone does not resolve child/teen privacy or consent requirements. The staging implementation and guardian onboarding must be reviewed against this rule before real minor enrollment; this draft is not authorization to enroll real minors.

Restricted age records can contain a birth date and review references; ordinary profile and Family responses do not expose raw birth dates. There is no public age-document upload or active identity/guardian verification provider in this build. Synthetic staging age records do not prove real-world eligibility.

Family Center stores linking invitations, participant account IDs, confirmations, settings, permission requests, decisions and audit history. Both participants have linking steps. This agreement is not proof of legal guardianship. Guardian controls can restrict eligible messaging and activities; they do not open private messages, confidential reports or otherwise inaccessible content. Teens can independently block and report. Ending supervision ends authority and permissions, not the stored history.

OWNER REVIEW: The 18+ standard-account / 13–17 supervised-teen / under-13 exclusion rule is owner-approved. Review its complete enforcement, launch jurisdictions, age assurance, guardian verification, consent, correction/dispute procedures and handling of children's information before real minor enrollment.

Photos and other media

Supported new JPEG and PNG uploads are decoded and re-encoded before storage, discarding unnecessary embedded metadata such as EXIF/GPS. Uploads that cannot be safely processed are rejected. This does not remove locations visible in the picture or written in a caption, and does not retroactively sanitize existing media.

Media is stored in the application's object storage and served through LINK authorization checks. Current upload limits exclude several camera formats; video upload is disabled. Existing Reel/Story interfaces are not a promise that new video uploads work or that video metadata is sanitized. This build has no general identity-document collection service.

Authentication and device storage

LINK uses secure, HttpOnly session cookies and stores token digests on the server. A session currently has a seven-day maximum lifetime; closing a tab does not itself sign you out. Browser settings can remove cookies sooner. Separate devices can have separate sessions. Signing out revokes the current session, and LINK's password-reset flow revokes existing LINK sessions before changing the provider password.

Short-lived enrollment and authentication-return cookies support account setup and returning to a requested page. Manual-area preferences use browser storage. LINK does not store raw passwords in localStorage or prefill demo passwords. A browser or device password manager may independently offer saved credentials under your control.

Authentication rate limits use hashed identifiers derived from submitted email and trusted network address information. Application safety logs and provider-call diagnostics support abuse prevention and troubleshooting. The Google adapter logs call type, safe outcome and duration, not precise coordinates, search text or credentials. Hosting/provider systems may separately process network and service information under their own policies; their complete logging and retention settings require owner confirmation.

Service providers and Google

  • Supabase: authentication, email verification and password recovery using the separate staging project. Application posts, messages and media remain in LINK storage rather than being moved into Supabase by this integration.
  • ChatGPT Sites and Cloudflare: hosting and the separate staging access gate, with Cloudflare D1 for application records and R2 for media. Hosting access and LINK account authentication are distinct layers.
  • Google Maps Platform: the implemented Places API integration can retrieve places on an explicit search, details on opening a place and one photo on request. Its optional Google map requires separate configuration and loads when you choose to show it. These Google features are not yet live-verified. A Google map/image request connects your browser to Google and can convey network/browser information and the displayed area; LINK's server-side Places requests convey the selected search area and query.
  • OpenStreetMap: the existing native-content map loads OpenStreetMap tiles. Tile requests convey the map area and browser/network information to the tile service. Google place results use a separate Google map, not this map.
  • Ticketmaster: an existing server-side integration can retrieve public event information when configured. A ticket or venue link opens the provider's service; this phase does not add a ticket checkout or transmit a LINK password to that service.

Google-provided features and content are subject to the then-current Google Maps End User Additional Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Google's Privacy Policy describes Google's handling of information for these features and is incorporated here for that use. LINK does not own Google content. Google and relevant contributor attribution remain attached to provider content.

Current Google results are used in transient application state, not copied into LINK's persistent business directory or media store. Place IDs can be included in shared links. LINK keeps usage counters and short-lived hashed request fingerprints to limit repeated calls, without saving raw search coordinates/text in those counters. Opening third-party websites, images or directions may disclose information to those providers under their own policies.

OWNER REVIEW: Confirm provider agreements, operating regions, international transfers, provider retention and any additional disclosures or choices needed for the actual launch configuration.

Your controls and deletion

Existing controls include editing profile fields and interests, choosing available audiences, block/mute and reversal, removing saved items or follows, declining requests, signing out, password recovery and managing Family settings or ending supervision. Browser settings let you revoke geolocation permission and clear local preferences/cookies. Available controls can be narrowed by age or safety rules.

Deletion is feature-specific. The build includes owner-authorized Reel and Highlight deletion and group-management removal/deletion controls. It does not provide a complete self-service account deletion/export process, general Post/Story deletion UI, direct-message deletion flow or a media-erasure service. Removing a content record or Highlight does not establish that every associated media object, copy or historical record has been erased.

Stories normally stop active viewing after 24 hours, with separate archive/Highlight behavior. That visibility limit is not a promise of deletion from storage. Signing out, blocking someone or ending supervision also does not erase account history or copies someone already obtained.

OWNER REVIEW: Establish a usable contact channel, verified account-closure and privacy-request procedure, export scope, deletion/redaction rules, provider coordination, exceptions and response timelines. No contact address or completion deadline is invented in this draft.

Retention and security

The application uses server-side authorization, restricted account/session checks, audience checks, bounded requests and upload validation. These controls reduce risk; they do not guarantee absolute security or prevent screenshots and onward sharing by an authorized recipient.

OWNER REVIEW: Retention depends on the type of information and operational, safety, security and legal requirements. LINK may need to retain limited information for security, fraud prevention, safety/reporting, legal obligations and backups. A formal retention schedule is not yet approved. Set retention and deletion rules for accounts, messages, media, reports, age evidence, Family history, security/audit records, backups and provider data, including any necessary legal holds. Technical session or Story expiry is not a substitute for those decisions.
