Status and service

LINK brings discovery and social features together so users can find people, places, activities and experiences, share content, and organize groups and plans. The current version is an isolated staging application with private access controls. Features may be incomplete, restricted or awaiting provider configuration.

These are draft terms for owner review, not a claim that a final contract has been adopted. LINK is referred to as the service/operator in this draft; no formal corporation or LLC identity is asserted. The formal operator identity, effective date and final acceptance process have not been approved. Read the Privacy draft for current information handling and unresolved privacy decisions.

Accounts and eligibility

LINK uses an independently authenticated account with email verification and a permanent LINK profile mapping. Use an email you control, protect your credentials, and do not impersonate another person or claim someone else's legacy account. The private hosting gate is separate from LINK authentication and does not establish ownership of a LINK profile.

Eligibility can vary by activity, group and account classification. The owner-approved policy requires standard LINK accounts to be 18+. Teens ages 13–17 may use LINK only through the Family/Teen structure with the required guardian relationship and applicable teen protections. Children under 13 are not eligible. This decision is not by itself proof of compliance with age, privacy or consent laws. Staging enforcement and guardian onboarding still require verification before real minor enrollment. Unknown age does not establish adulthood or eligibility for restricted activities.

Family linking requires participant confirmations but is not verified legal guardianship. Guardian approval does not override platform restrictions, blocks or activity eligibility. Do not use synthetic test identities or age records as evidence about a real person.

OWNER REVIEW: Verify complete enforcement of the approved eligibility rule and approve launch jurisdictions, age assurance, guardian consent/verification and the final account/terms acceptance process before public enrollment, especially enrollment of real minors.

Acceptable use — proposed rules

Use LINK respectfully and lawfully. These proposed rules require owner approval before adoption:

  • Do not threaten, harass, exploit, impersonate or discriminate against other people, or post material that violates their privacy or rights.
  • Do not publish another person's confidential location or personal information without appropriate permission. Never use LINK to exploit or endanger a child.
  • Do not send spam, scams or deceptive invitations, misrepresent an activity or verification status, or abuse reporting and approval tools.
  • Do not bypass account, audience, group, age, message-request or upload restrictions, or use another account to evade a block.
  • Do not scrape provider content, remove required attribution, interfere with the service or evade rate and usage limits.

Use the existing report, block and mute tools where relevant. Having a direct or shared link does not authorize access to private content.

Your content and intellectual property

Posts, photos, Stories, Reels, comments, messages, groups, activities, plans and poll contributions are user-provided content. Choose the audience carefully and make sure you have appropriate rights and permission to upload or share what you provide. Authorized recipients may be able to copy or share information outside LINK.

Users retain ownership of content they create. By providing content, users give LINK the permissions reasonably necessary to host, process, display and distribute that content as part of operating LINK, subject to its audience and access rules. No ownership transfer or unnecessarily broad perpetual license is intended; final legal license wording remains OWNER REVIEW. LINK does not claim ownership of Google or other third-party content; their rights and required attribution remain with the relevant owners.

OWNER REVIEW: Approve the operator's intellectual-property statement, the specific license needed to host/display user content, its duration and termination, and a usable copyright/rights complaint process. No broad perpetual license or unsupported corporate ownership claim is inserted here.

Activities, plans and real-world interactions

Users supply activity and plan descriptions, schedules, venues, capacity, rules and invitations. A listing, RSVP, saved item, poll result or guardian approval does not guarantee attendance, admission, a reservation, payment, venue suitability or physical supervision.

Check the host, venue, current schedule, access requirements and any separately stated charges before attending. Respect activity rules, age restrictions, audience choices and another person's decision to leave or decline. Hosts should keep their information accurate and use available cancellation/member controls when plans change.

Use care when meeting people or sharing locations. Trust history, recognition photos, email verification and Family approvals are limited signals, not background checks or guarantees of safety. LINK is not an emergency response service. Contact local emergency services when there is immediate danger.

OWNER REVIEW: Determine any final meetup-risk language, consumer protections, host obligations, payment/refund arrangements and allocation of legal responsibility. This draft does not invent a liability waiver or cap.

Safety, reporting and moderation

Users may report content and accounts. LINK may restrict or remove content or accounts consistent with its implemented moderation and safety systems. LINK provides reports, blocks, mutes, Safety Center, Trust Profiles and Family controls. Hosts and authorized group managers have role-specific tools, including member removal/restriction. Authorized safety reviewers can review reports and record decisions. A report marked actioned does not by itself establish wrongdoing or automatically suspend an account.

Backend account status and session revocation can prevent access to disabled or suspended accounts. These technical controls are not a completed moderation staffing, appeal or enforcement policy. Reporting does not promise a response time, round-the-clock monitoring, rescue or a particular outcome.

Guardians cannot read private teen messages or confidential reports just because of their relationship. Teens can independently use safety tools. Approval cannot bypass hard age rules or a block.

OWNER REVIEW: Approve prohibited-content definitions, review procedures, enforcement grounds, notices, appeals, emergency/escalation handling and lawful information-request procedures. Define how violations of Google's applicable terms will be handled, including restriction of Google features where required.

Third-party places, events and maps

LINK's current implementation includes Google Places features/content and an optional Google map awaiting configuration. Use of Google Maps features and content is subject to the then-current Google Maps End User Additional Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy, incorporated for that use. Do not remove Google/contributor attribution or extract, bulk-copy or rehost Google content outside permitted use.

Google information is separate from LINK-native user content. Place categories, ratings, photos, hours and availability may be missing or out of date and are not LINK endorsements or guarantees. The current search returns a limited set, not every place in an area. Confirm details directly with the venue. Map and direction information is not a substitute for observing actual conditions and local requirements.

The existing native-content map uses OpenStreetMap tiles with attribution. An existing Ticketmaster integration can supply public events when configured. Website, ticket and directions links take you to external services whose terms and privacy practices apply. LINK's current flow does not itself sell tickets or establish a booking merely because a user follows a link.

OWNER REVIEW: Confirm the actual provider agreements, launch configuration and any future commercial/affiliate relationships. No sponsorship, partnership, payment service or endorsement is invented by this draft.

Availability, changes and account closure

Staging is for testing. Requests can fail, content can become unavailable, and features may be limited by authentication, privacy, eligibility, provider availability or usage limits. New video upload is currently disabled. No uninterrupted-service or absolute-security guarantee is made.

You can sign out, change available profile/audience settings, remove saved items/follows, use supported feature-specific deletion controls and end Family supervision. Signing out ends the current session; it does not delete your account. Other device sessions can remain valid unless revoked or expired. A successful LINK password-reset journey revokes existing LINK sessions.

A complete self-service account-closure/export process and coordinated data erasure are not implemented. LINK may need to retain limited information for security, fraud prevention, safety/reporting, legal obligations and backups. Retention depends on information type and operational, safety, security and legal requirements; no fixed period is promised and the formal schedule remains OWNER REVIEW. Content removal, Story expiry and ending supervision do not guarantee deletion of all stored records or media. See the Privacy draft's controls and deletion section.

OWNER REVIEW: Establish account closure, termination grounds/process, retention/deletion exceptions and timelines, material service-change notices and effective-date procedures. Do not treat these drafts as the final closure or termination policy.
